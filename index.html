Destinations & experiences

Six ways to spend an Arctic day.

Every one is private — your own vehicle and driver-guide, hotel pickup in Tromsø, and a day shaped around your group rather than a timetable.

Scenic day tours
All year · 5–6 h

Sommarøy — the Arctic Caribbean

White coral-sand beaches and turquoise water an hour from Tromsø. We drive the scenic route across Kvaløya, past Ersfjord and the open coast, with time to walk the beaches and stop wherever the light is good.

  • Kvaløya coastal road, fjord and beach photo stops
  • Time for coffee or lunch on the island
  • Shortest day tour — ideal for families
Tromsø → Kvaløya → Sommarøy
Request this tour
All year · 10–12 h

Senja — Norway in miniature

Norway's second-largest island packs sheer peaks, fishing villages and white beaches into one coastline. A long, rewarding day along the national scenic route, at your pace.

  • Views of Segla, Tungeneset and Bergsbotn viewpoints
  • Ersfjord beach and the fishing villages of the outer coast
  • A full day — best for guests who love landscapes and photography
Tromsø → Senja scenic route → Tromsø
Request this tour
All year · 7–9 h

Lyngen Alps — peaks above the fjord

Glaciated summits rising straight out of the sea. We cross the Ullsfjord by ferry and follow the Lyngenfjord shoreline, with stops under some of Northern Norway's highest mountains.

  • Fjord ferry crossing included in the route
  • Alpine scenery, quiet villages and shoreline photo stops
  • Also a favourite dark-sky area for aurora in winter
Tromsø → Breivikeidet ferry → Lyngen

Arctic wildlife & culture
Nov–Apr · Half day

Private husky experience

Meet the dogs, learn how a team works, and head out across the snow by sled. We arrange your session with a trusted local kennel and drive you there and back privately.

  • Private transport from your hotel — no shared bus
  • Drive your own sled or ride as a passenger
  • Warm suits and boots provided at the kennel
Tromsø → Husky kennel → Tromsø
Request this tour
Nov–Jan · Full day

Private whale watching

Each winter, orcas and humpbacks follow the herring into the fjords north of Tromsø. We take you to the whales in comfort and arrange your place on a boat with a responsible, licensed operator.

  • Private drive north through fjord landscapes
  • Orcas and humpback whales in their natural habitat
  • Wild animals — sightings are likely in season, never guaranteed
Tromsø → Skjervøy whale fjords → Tromsø
Request this tour
Nov–Apr · Half day

Private reindeer & Sami visit

Feed the reindeer by hand, sit by the fire in a lavvu and hear Sami stories and joik from the people whose herd it is. A calm, warm experience that works for every age.

  • Reindeer feeding and optional sledding
  • Sami culture, storytelling and a traditional meal
  • Easy to combine with a northern lights evening
Tromsø → Sami reindeer camp → Tromsø
